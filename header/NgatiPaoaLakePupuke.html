NgatiPaoa

Ngāti Pāoa is a Māori iwi of the Hauraki region, with traditional lands stretching from the western Hauraki Plains to Auckland and settlements on Hauraki Gulf islands like Waiheke. It is one of five tribes in the Marutūāhu confederation, alongside Ngāti Maru, Ngāti Rongoū, Ngāti Tamaterā, and Ngāti Whanaunga. Descended from Marutūāhu, son of Hotunui, who arrived on the Tainui canoe, these tribes belong to the Tainui group and the wider Hauraki collective.

Lake Pupuke

Lake Pupuke, traditionally known in Māori as Pupukemoana, is a heart-shaped freshwater lake in a volcanic crater (maar) east of Westlake Boys High School. Its shape comes from two linked craters—a larger one forming most of the lake and a smaller one creating the northeast arm. Separated from the sea by less than 200 m, it has a 4.5 km circumference and reaches 57 m deep. The lake is popular for recreation and its waterfront properties.
